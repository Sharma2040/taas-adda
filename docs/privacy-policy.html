Taas Adda Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026, updated for ads · App: Taas Adda (com.taasadda.app)

Taas Adda is a free card game app. It has no accounts and no analytics of its own, and it does not send your information to us or to any server we run. It shows ads from Google AdMob, as described below.

What stays on your phone

Your player name, avatar, settings, game statistics, levels, coins, achievements and the emergency contact numbers you type in are saved only on your device. Coins can't be bought or exchanged for money. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this.

Playing with friends nearby

When you host or join a nearby table, phones connect directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi using Google Play services (Nearby Connections). Your player name, game moves, chat messages and emoji go only to the other phones at your table. They are not stored after the table closes.

Android requires the Nearby devices permission for this, or Location on Android 12 and older, because Bluetooth scanning can reveal location. Taas Adda does not read, record or upload your location for nearby play.

SOS feature

Your location is read only when you press an SOS button with “Include my current location” turned on. It is then sent only where you choose:

  • Alert everyone at this table: your message and location go directly to the phones at your nearby table.
  • Text my emergency contacts: the app opens your phone’s messaging app with the message and location filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send there.
  • Emergency numbers: the app opens your phone’s dialer with the number filled in.

Taas Adda does not contact emergency services itself and does not keep your location.

Ads

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob: optional video ads you choose to watch for bonus coins, and occasional ads between full games, never during a hand. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about ad interactions and app performance. Google's use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ads and privacy → Privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for teens, and gambling ads are blocked.

Children

Taas Adda is not directed at children under 13. Some games use pretend chips for betting; there is no real money and nothing can be bought.

Changes and contact
